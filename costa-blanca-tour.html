Canelobre Cave sets the tone for the tour

  • +Explore Canelobre Cave inside Upper Jurassic limestone formed around 145 million years ago.
  • +Stand beneath a huge 70-meter vault, often noted as one of the highest cave chambers in Spain.
  • +Learn how the cave connects to the Spanish Civil War as well as to the geology of the area.
  • +Finish in Busot, where the tour slows down into village views, church architecture, and castle ruins.
